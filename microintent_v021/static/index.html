MicroIntent

A portfolio prototype exploring whether conversational AI can match a small number of clearly labeled sponsored recommendations to the exact sub-intent where they are useful.

Independent concept · v0.3.1
Public demo: runs the transparent rule-based extractor, with no LLM calls. Gemini structured extraction is available when you run it locally — see the repository.
Try a sample or start a conversation. Follow up with preferences such as “black leather”, “under $100”, or “must fit a Kindle” to see current-session context accumulate.
